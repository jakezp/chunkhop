Support

Start with the app’s Help & FAQs

Keep your phone and console on the same reachable IPv4 LAN. A wired console is fine. The destination must be reachable by the console independently of the phone.

If no entry appears, check guest isolation, VPN routing, multicast/broadcast filtering and other apps using the discovery ports. Connection counters show what the phone receives and sends; they do not prove the console received a reply.
