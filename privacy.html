Privacy policy

ChunkHop · Effective 18 October 2026

About this policy

ChunkHop is an Android LAN connection companion for Minecraft Bedrock, provided by jakezp. This policy explains the data handled by the app. Contact: jakezp@gmail.com. Effective date: 18 October 2026.

No developer collection or tracking

ChunkHop has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend. The app does not send your information to the developer, sell your data or use your data for advertising.

Saved on your phone

The destination server address and UDP port you enter are saved in the app’s private storage so you can use them again. Android backup is disabled for this app. You can replace these values in the app, or remove all saved app data by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Temporary local connection data

While sharing, the app uses your Wi-Fi IPv4 subnet, local device addresses, discovery messages and temporary Minecraft connection/login data to advertise a LAN entry and transfer your console. Local diagnostics show counters, source addresses and recent connection errors. This information is processed on your device in memory; the app does not upload it to the developer or write a session-history database. Stopping sharing closes the listeners and temporary connections. Diagnostics can remain in memory until another session replaces them or the app process closes.

Your chosen server and network

The destination address and port are sent to your console as part of the transfer. Your console then connects directly to that server; ChunkHop does not relay gameplay. The destination server operator, Minecraft platform services, network provider and DNS provider handle their own traffic under their own policies. Local discovery broadcasts are visible to devices on the same network; they are not a private messaging channel.

Android permissions and background sharing

Internet/network access and multicast support enable LAN discovery and the initial connection. Network-state access identifies the usable Wi-Fi subnet. The connected-device foreground service keeps sharing available when you switch apps, and the notification shows its status and Stop action. The app does not request contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location permissions. Android can stop background processes under its own rules.

Optional tips and external links

The optional $1 USD thank-you link opens PayPal in your browser. It does not unlock features, and the app does not collect card details or payment credentials. PayPal and your browser handle information you provide under their own policies. If you contact the developer by email or complete a tip, the information you voluntarily send is handled through those external services, separately from the app’s local connection processing.

Support, deletion and platform services

There is no ChunkHop account or developer-held app database to delete. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove saved settings. Avoid including passwords, authentication tokens or unredacted addresses in a public support report. Android, Google Play, your browser and other services may process their own information independently of ChunkHop.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app’s data handling changes. The current policy is available in the app and on this website. For questions about this policy or information you have voluntarily sent to the developer, email jakezp@gmail.com.
